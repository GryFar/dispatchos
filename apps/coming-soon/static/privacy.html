Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

1. Who we are

This site is operated by DispatchOS LLC (in formation), Clearwater, Florida, USA. Contact: privacy@dispatchos.co.

2. What we collect on this coming-soon page

When you submit your email to the launch list, we store:

  • Your email address
  • Submission timestamp (UTC)
  • IP address and country (derived from request, used for rate-limiting and basic fraud prevention)
  • User-Agent string (browser/OS identifier sent automatically by your browser)
  • Referrer URL, if any

We do not use cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics on this page. We do not sell your data.

3. Why we use it

The email list is used solely to notify you when DispatchOS launches and to share product updates relevant to dispatch operations. IP/UA metadata is used only for abuse prevention.

4. Where it lives

Subscriber records are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV (US/EU edge regions). Email is routed through Cloudflare Email Routing to a Gmail inbox controlled by the founder.

5. How long we keep it

Subscriber records are retained until the product launches, plus 24 months thereafter, unless you request earlier deletion.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@dispatchos.co. We will respond within 30 days. EU/UK and California residents have additional rights under GDPR / CCPA respectively, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will email subscribers and update the date above before applying changes.
